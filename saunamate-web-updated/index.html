COLD & RECOVERY

Find your balance after the heat

Cold exposure is optional, not a competition. Start gently and choose a temperature that feels manageable. You can enjoy a sauna ritual without a cold plunge.

Before you begin

Hydrate, check how you're feeling and choose a safe, supervised place to cool down.

Take it gradually

Try fresh air or a lukewarm shower before considering colder water. Never jump into unknown water.

Recover & rest

Warm up comfortably, take a break and listen to your body rather than chasing a target time.

Safety note: Cold immersion can place stress on the heart and circulation. Avoid breath-holding or hyperventilating in water. If you have a heart or circulatory condition, are pregnant, or have medical concerns, ask a qualified clinician before trying cold immersion. Stop if you feel dizzy, faint or unwell.

CALM & RELAXATION

Make room for the quieter moments

A good wellness ritual doesn't need to be complicated. A few intentional minutes before and after the sauna can help you slow down and reconnect.

Settle in

Put your phone away, sit comfortably and take a moment to notice your surroundings.

Breathe naturally

Keep your breathing relaxed and unforced. Step outside if you feel overheated or uncomfortable.

Keep the calm going

Drink some water, allow time to cool down and enjoy a quiet transition back into your day.
